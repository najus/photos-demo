Gallery, reimagined

Six experiments, all running on your real 90 Flickr photos. Scroll through, play with the controls, and pick your favourites.

01

Story hero

A cinematic opener, like the timeline's story mode: slow Ken Burns zoom, segment bars, auto-advance — hold the photo to pause.

02

Editorial album index

Albums as a big-type table of contents. On desktop, hover a row — a preview follows your cursor. (The trick personal sites like paco.me made famous.)

03

The grid playground

One grid, many personalities. Switch layout, density, and what a hover reveals. Every tile is tinted with its photo's real dominant colour while it loads — and glows with it in Darkroom mode.

Click any photo for experiment 05 — the lightbox.

04

Filmstrip

The Airplanes album as a roll of film — sprocket holes, frame numbers, drag or scroll sideways. Pure nostalgia for the film era, CSS-only.

06

Colour story

Your whole archive sorted by its dominant colour — a rainbow index. Photographers use this as a surprisingly beautiful way to browse. Click a swatch-photo to open it.

The menu

  1. Story hero — cinematic auto-playing opener
  2. Editorial album index — big-type rows + cursor-following previews
  3. Grid playground — masonry / justified / square, density, EXIF hover, colour tinting
  4. Filmstrip — horizontal film-roll album view
  5. Lightbox Pro — ambient colour glow, filmstrip thumbs, EXIF panel (click any photo)
  6. Colour story — browse the archive sorted by dominant colour
  7. Darkroom mode — the 🌑 toggle up top restyles the whole page
